About Our Comic

Our comic is about the importance of taking care of our environment. It shows how improper waste disposal and pollution can harm nature and affect people's lives.

Through this story, we want to remind everyone that protecting the environment is everyone's responsibility. Simple actions like throwing garbage properly, recycling, and keeping our surroundings clean can make a big difference.

Integration of Different Subjects

Mathematics

Polynomials as Trash and Finding the Solution

We use polynomials to represent waste and solve mathematical problems that help us understand waste management.

Araling Panlipunan

Taking Action Against River Pollution

A child sees trash floating in the river and wants to do something to help, but feels powerless to stop the problem. This shows how environmental issues affect communities and why people need to work together to protect nature.

Science

Plastic Pollution and Proper Waste Segregation

Plastic waste harms animals and pollutes the water. Through science, we learn why proper waste segregation is important in protecting living organisms and keeping our environment clean.
